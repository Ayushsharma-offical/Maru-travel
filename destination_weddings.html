01 — RAJASTHAN

JAIPUR

The Pink City — A Royal Celebration

Accessibility, heritage and convenience make Jaipur India's most compelling wedding destination. Pink palaces, Aravalli backdrops and world-class luxury hotels create stunning settings.

  • Taj Rambagh Palace
  • Jai Mahal Palace
  • Samode Palace
  • Jaipur Marriott
02 — RAJASTHAN

JODHPUR

Where Royalty Lives On

Experience Rajasthan's richest royal legacy. Historic palaces and grand havelis where modern luxury blends seamlessly with centuries of tradition for a truly majestic celebration.

  • Umaid Bhawan
  • Ajit Bhawan
  • Vivanta Hari Mahal
  • Indana Palace
03 — RAJASTHAN

KUMBHALGARH

A Fairytale Within the Aravallis

Steeped in royal legacy, this is an extraordinary setting for unforgettable celebrations. Magnificent fort architecture, traditional cuisine and dramatic landscapes.

  • Devi Palace Resort
  • The Aodhi
  • Dera Kumbhalgarh
  • Forest Retreat
04 — RAJASTHAN

UDAIPUR

Romance in Every Frame

Shimmering lakes, magnificent palaces and the Aravallis create the world's most naturally romantic wedding backdrop. The iconic waterfront settings are simply unmatched.

  • Taj Lake Palace
  • Oberoi Udaivilas
  • The Leela Palace
  • Trident Udaipur
05 — DELHI NCR

DELHI

Heritage Meets Contemporary Luxury

India's capital delivers exceptional connectivity and world-class event infrastructure. Vintage havelis and heritage hotels create unforgettable, grand weddings.

  • Palm Greens
  • Victorian Palace
  • Shangri-La Eros
  • Crowne Plaza
